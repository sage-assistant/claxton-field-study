The Synopsis

Every Legend Has an Origin

In 2003, a young visionary looked out over the coast of Cape Town and made a decision that would alter the course of enterprise IT forever. Armed with nothing but calm focus, a surfboard, and what sources describe as "an unreasonable belief in Microsoft products," Michael Claxton co-founded Solid Systems. Within years, his influence had spread to Johannesburg. Then London. Now, with over two decades of managed services, cloud migrations, and human connections elevated, the world faces a question it was never prepared to answer: what happens when one man becomes too good at IT consulting?
